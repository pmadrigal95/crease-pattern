This is the personal site of Pablo Madrigal, an individual based in Costa Rica. It sells nothing, has no
accounts and no forms. If you have a question about this page, write to the email address in the footer.

## The short version

- **No cookies.** This site sets none, and neither does anything it loads.
- **No profiles.** Visits are counted in aggregate. Nothing here can tell that two visits came from the same
  person.
- **Post counts are totals.** Each Precrease post shows how many times it was opened, read to the end and
  marked as helpful. Only those three numbers are stored.
- **No ads, no third-party trackers, no data sold or shared** for marketing.
- **Your browser's privacy signals are respected.** With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track turned on,
  the analytics script is never loaded, and posts don't report your view or your read.

## What happens when you visit

**Hosting.** The site is served by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Like any web server, Cloudflare
receives the technical data every request carries, such as your IP address, browser and the page you asked
for, and processes it to deliver and protect the site under the
[Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). This site keeps no request logs of its
own.

**Analytics.** To know which pages are read and whether the site loads fast, it uses
[Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/). After the page loads, a small script
from `static.cloudflareinsights.com` sends one report to `cloudflareinsights.com` with:

- the page you viewed and the page that linked to it;
- your country, browser, operating system and device type;
- how fast the page loaded and how stable it was while loading.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors. I
only ever see totals, such as views per page or visits per country, and I don't copy them anywhere else.
Cloudflare is a U.S. company and may process this data outside your country, as described in its privacy
policy.

**Post counts.** On a Precrease post, the page tells this site's own server (`/api/stats`) three things,
each as a bare event with no identifier:

- that the post was opened;
- that you reached the end after spending some time on it;
- that you pressed "This helped", if you do.

The server adds one to that post's total and keeps nothing else: no IP address, no time of the event, no
browser details. To stop one person from inflating the numbers, Cloudflare's rate limiter looks at your IP
address in memory for about a minute and discards it; it is never written down. The totals are public, under
each post. With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track on, the first two events are not sent.

**Sharing.** The LinkedIn, X and Bluesky buttons under a post are plain links. Nothing from those services
loads until you click one, and then you are on their site, under their privacy policy.

**Comments.** Posts have a "Show comments" button. Until you press it, nothing about comments loads. When
you do, the page loads [giscus](https://giscus.app/), which shows the post's thread from this site's GitHub
Discussions: your browser then contacts `giscus.app` and GitHub, under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Reading needs no account. To comment, you sign in with GitHub, and giscus keeps that sign-in inside its own
comment frame, not on this site. Your comment is public, on GitHub, under your GitHub name.

**Preferences.** The site reads three values from your browser's local storage: `pm.direction` and
`pm.theme`, to show the visual identity and theme you picked, and `pm.helped`, the posts where you pressed
"This helped", so the button doesn't count you twice. They are written only when you do those things, they
never leave your device, and clearing your site data removes them.

**Fonts and everything else** are served from this site itself. No other third party is contacted unless you
open the comments or follow a share link.

## Why, and your rights

Counting visits in aggregate and keeping the site fast and secure are legitimate interests of running a public
site (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR; Costa Rica's Law 8968 on personal data protection). Under those laws you
can ask to access, correct or delete personal data and object to its processing. Because the analytics are
aggregate, there is usually nothing that can be linked back to you, but write to me and I'll answer. You can
also complain to your data protection authority: in Costa Rica, the
[PRODHAB](https://www.prodhab.go.cr/); in the EU, the authority of your country.

## Check it yourself

Open your browser's developer tools on any page:

- **Application → Cookies:** empty.
- **Application → Local storage:** empty, or only `pm.direction`, `pm.theme` and `pm.helped` if you used
  them.
- **Network:** requests to this site, including `/api/stats` on posts, plus `static.cloudflareinsights.com`
  and `cloudflareinsights.com` unless your browser sends a privacy signal.

If anything you see doesn't match this page, that's a bug. Please tell me.
